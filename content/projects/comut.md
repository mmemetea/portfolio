In 2004, fleet management ran on phone calls and paper logbooks. Com-UT replaced both.

**The product:**
- Real-time vehicle tracking over GPRS
- CANbus integration for deep vehicle diagnostics
- Driver behavior analytics: speeding, harsh braking, fuel consumption
- Predictive maintenance scheduled from vehicle telemetry
- A MongoDB pipeline handling thousands of events per second

**The outcome:**
- From founding team to 28 people
- €3M annual recurring revenue, across logistics, construction and public transport
- Acquired by a French industrial group; the team and technology moved into its digital transformation division
