Public-transport tenders were answered with about a month of Excel per network: timetables, battery capacity, opportunity charging, all by hand. Replaced with an OptaPlanner (Timefold) model that schedules several hundred e-buses in under seven seconds.

The model handles transportation networks, timetables, battery capacity and opportunity-charging constraints, including battery degradation, charging infrastructure limits, and passenger demand across heterogeneous fleets.

Built as a product for Daimler's public transport division (EvoBus), enabling real-time digital planning that transformed how transport operators responded to RFPs. The product became a core differentiator for tb.lx in the public transport market.
