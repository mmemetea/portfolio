Bike alarms bolt on, blink, and get unscrewed. OWLO went the other way: the whole alarm
disappears inside the seat post, where a thief never thinks to look.

**What it took:**
- A custom PCB around a SIGFOX module: alerts reach several kilometers, where a thief would already be gone
- BLE pairing and a mobile app for configuration, geolocation and status
- Tamper detection that fires the moment the post is disturbed
- Firmware on an ARM Cortex-M, tuned for 1+ year of battery life

**Where it got:**
- Finalist at Shark Tank Portugal (season 2, 2016), finalist at the ENGIE Singapore OpenInnov Challenge
- Showcased at the Productized hardware workshop

Finalist, not a shipped product. Listed because the build is real.
