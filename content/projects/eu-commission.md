Contributed to the European Commission's tobacco track-and-trace initiative as a freelance technical consultant. The project was part of the broader EU Tobacco Products Directive (TPD) implementation.

**Scope of Work:**
- Technical research on track-and-trace system architectures
- Policy analysis supporting the EU's illicit trade monitoring framework
- Evaluation of serialization and authentication technologies
- Cross-border coordination analysis across member states

**Deliverables:**
- Contributed to the official EU report on track-and-trace feasibility studies
- Technical recommendations for the EU-wide system specification

The resulting framework became a reference for member states implementing national track-and-trace systems.
