AutoSeverity is a clinical decision-support system built for Luz Saúde: patient severity scored automatically from electronic health record data, surfaced to clinicians through a real-time indicators dashboard.

**Key work:**
- Discovery and indicator mapping with clinical stakeholders
- Healthcare interoperability via HL7 and FHIR, with LOINC-coded labs
- Architecture proposal and Figma-designed clinician UI
